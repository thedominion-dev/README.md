# The Dominion

Small, practical tools for streamers and creators. Made by Jason Duncan, operating as The Dominion.

## Pillage

**Pillage** finds the best moments in your Twitch VODs or your own video files, cuts them to vertical or horizontal, and burns in captions. It runs on your own Windows PC. There is no account, no telemetry, and nothing is uploaded.

- **Lite** is free.
- **Pro** is a one-time purchase with no subscription. It adds webcam layouts, a keep/reject/trim review page, caption preview, post titles and hashtags, your logo on every clip, and Stream Markers and Twitch Clips support. It includes all 1.x updates.
